Premium Workshop, Honest Service

Noor Mechanical Engineering Services Est. has been Dubai's benchmark for automotive excellence since 2009. Located in Ras Al Khor Industrial Area 1, we combine cutting-edge diagnostic equipment with old-school craftsmanship and integrity.

From routine oil changes to full engine rebuilds, our team of certified specialists delivers results you can trust — with transparent pricing and genuine parts, every time.
